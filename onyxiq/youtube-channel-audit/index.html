What the audit examines

  • Upload consistency and cadence. Gaps, bursts and silent months — the rhythm YouTube and your subscribers actually experience, measured instead of remembered.
  • Recent performance vs. your own baseline. Not vanity totals: whether your latest videos beat, match or trail what your channel normally does.
  • Winners and drag. Your strongest videos and your weakest, side by side — because the gap between them is usually a pattern (topic, title style, length), not luck.
  • Title and keyword patterns. Whether your titles target phrases people actually search, or are written only for existing subscribers.
  • Catalogue balance. How much of your library works for discovery (search and suggested) versus how much only serves people who already found you.

All of it rolls up into one health score — and unlike a bare number, the score ships with the findings that produced it, in the order you should fix them.

How to run your audit

  1. Connect. Open OnyxIQ and sign in with your own Google account, or paste your own free YouTube Data API key. The audit runs on the official YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics — no scraping, no password sharing.
  2. Run Channel X-Ray. Point it at your channel and let it read your uploads, titles and performance data.
  3. Read the findings, not just the score. Work from the top: the audit ranks what's costing you the most views.
  4. Fix, publish, re-audit. Change one thing at a time — cadence, title style, topic focus — then re-run the audit next month and see whether the score (and the views) moved.

Good to know (honest limits)

  • The health score is a guide built from your channel's public and account API data — it summarises patterns; it is not YouTube's internal judgement of your channel, and no tool outside YouTube can show you that.
  • An audit can't fix a channel by itself. It tells you where the leaks are; plugging them is still your next few uploads.
  • Very new or very small channels have thin data — the audit will say so rather than inventing precision.
  • Your data is processed on your machine through your own access. Connect with your own API key (entirely your own Google project) or Google sign-in (through the OnyxIQ project, whose Google verification is being completed).

Frequently asked questions

What does a YouTube channel audit actually check?

Channel X-Ray reviews your upload consistency and cadence, how your recent videos perform against your own average, which topics and title patterns earn views, whether your titles target searchable keywords, and how your catalogue is balanced — then distils it into a health score with the findings listed underneath.

Is the channel audit free?

You can start with OnyxIQ Basic, which is free forever. Prime — $49 once, no subscription — unlocks the full studio. Both run on your own free YouTube API access.

Does the audit need access to my channel?

It reads channel data through the official YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics using your own Google sign-in or your own API key. Your password is never asked for, and nothing is uploaded to an OnyxIQ server — there isn't one.

How often should I audit my channel?

Monthly is a good rhythm for most creators, and again after any deliberate change — a new format, a new schedule, a new niche direction — so you can see whether the change actually moved the numbers.
