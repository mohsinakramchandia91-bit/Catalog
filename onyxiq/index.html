The problem: renting your own growth, forever

The big YouTube tools are good — and they are rented. A modest monthly plan quietly becomes hundreds of dollars over the life of a channel — paid whether you upload or not. The moment you stop paying, the audits, the keyword data and the workflow you built your process around switch off. For a small or growing channel, that meter never stops running.

OnyxIQ takes the other side of that deal: it runs on your own free YouTube API access instead of our servers, so there is no infrastructure bill to pass back to you — so the tool can be sold the old-fashioned way: pay once, own it, use it as long as you make videos. And your channel's numbers are never uploaded to, stored on, or sold from a third-party server — everything runs on your own machine.

  • Channel X-Ray — a full channel audit with a health score: upload consistency, what's working, what's quietly leaking views.

  1. Get the tool. Download Basic free, or Prime for the full studio — both yours to keep.

Frequently asked questions

Is OnyxIQ really a one-time payment?

Yes. Prime is $49 once — no monthly or yearly renewal. Basic is free forever. The tool runs on your own free YouTube API access, so there is no server bill being passed on to you as a subscription.

Does OnyxIQ need my YouTube password?

No. You sign in with your own Google account or use your own free YouTube Data API key. OnyxIQ never asks for your password, and your channel data is processed on your machine — never uploaded anywhere, because there are no OnyxIQ servers.

How is OnyxIQ different from TubeBuddy or vidIQ?

They are established tools with big teams and years of proprietary data, rented monthly. OnyxIQ covers the core growth jobs — audits, keyword research, competitor comparison, thumbnail testing — for a single one-time payment, using your own API access so your data stays with you.

Will OnyxIQ guarantee my channel grows?

No tool can honestly promise that. OnyxIQ shows you where your channel is leaking views and which topics are worth making — but the videos, and the growth, are still yours to earn.
